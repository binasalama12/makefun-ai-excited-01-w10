# SODA GEYSER

It was supposed to be the boring part of the afternoon — one warm soda bottle in the backyard, a few mints dropped in, and everyone expecting a sad little fizz they could film and forget. Instead the bottle went off like a volcano, a white column shooting three metres straight up past her head, and she was standing exactly where she should not have been: close enough to get drenched, laughing the loudest of anyone, mouth wide open while her friends' hands flew in from the edges of the frame trying to shield themselves and failing completely. Nobody planned that face, nobody could have posed it — it is the half-second where a cheap experiment turned into the story they will retell all summer. The best part is that she never stepped back: soaked, sticky, and still grinning, she held her ground right next to the bottle as if she wanted the geyser to know it had picked the right person to erupt beside.

The photo was graded gently and stored as entry.png, and main.ts copies it to the output path while recording the SHA-256 of both source and copy so the result stays deterministic.

## Why it excites

- **Jolt:** A vertical geyser column erupts past the top of her head, turning a still portrait into a split-second explosion frozen mid-blast.
- **Surprise:** Everyone braced for a tiny fizz and got a three-metre volcano instead — her wide-open, full-throated laugh is the honest receipt of that gap.
- **Momentum:** Foam, spray, and flying hands all push outward from the bottle at once, so the whole frame feels like it is still moving even though it is frozen.
- **Thumbnail punch:** Her ecstatic face fills the frame right beside the bottle while backlit droplets sparkle like tiny diamonds in the golden light — readable and joyful even at the smallest size.
